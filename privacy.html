Privacy Policy

Coverly: Receipts & Warranty · Last updated 10 November 2026

Coverly is built so that your receipts stay yours. We don't run servers that store your data, we don't have accounts, and we don't use ads, analytics or tracking.

What Coverly stores, and where

Receipts you add (photo, store, product, date, total, warranty length and notes) are stored on your device. If you're signed in to iCloud, they are also stored in your own private iCloud database so they sync across your devices. Apple runs iCloud under Apple's privacy policy; the developer of Coverly cannot see your iCloud data.

Text recognition

Reading the store, date and total from a receipt happens entirely on your device using Apple's Vision framework. Receipt images are never uploaded for processing.

Backups

Backup files you export, and automatic backups to a folder you choose, are encrypted with a password you set and saved only where you choose (for example iCloud Drive). The automatic-backup password is kept in your device's Keychain and is not synced. We cannot recover a lost backup password.

Sharing

When you share a receipt photo or a Warranty Claim PDF, the file is created on your device and sent only to the app or person you pick in the share sheet.

Notifications

Warranty reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. No push server is involved.

Purchases

The optional one-time "Coverly Unlimited" purchase is handled by Apple through the App Store. We receive no payment details.

Data we collect

None. Coverly does not collect, sell or share personal data, and does not track you across apps or websites.

Children

Coverly is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Deleting your data

Delete receipts in the app, or delete the app. To remove the iCloud copy, go to Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Account Storage › Coverly on your iPhone.
