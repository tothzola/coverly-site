Common questions

The scan read the wrong date or total

Tap the field and correct it before saving. Coverly reads European formats (15.03.2026, 12 990 Ft, 89,98 €) on your device; a straight, well-lit photo works best.

I didn't get a reminder

Check Settings › Notifications › Coverly is allowed. Reminders arrive at 9:00 on the day 30 days and 7 days before the warranty ends.

My receipts aren't on my other iPhone or iPad

Both devices need to be signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud on. Open Coverly › Settings to see the iCloud Sync status. Large photos can take a few minutes to arrive.

How do I restore a backup?

Settings › Restore from Backup, pick the .coverly file and enter its password. Restoring only adds receipts that aren't already there and never needs a purchase. Older .keepsafe backups open too.

I forgot my backup password

Backups are encrypted so only you can open them, which means nobody can recover a lost password. Your receipts in the app and in iCloud are not affected; export a new backup with a new password.

How do I restore my purchase?

Settings › Coverly Unlimited › Restore. Family members get it through Family Sharing.
